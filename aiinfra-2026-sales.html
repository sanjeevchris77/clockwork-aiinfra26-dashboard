A standalone, sales-facing view of the AI Infra Summit 2026 attendee roster cross-referenced against Salesforce ABM tiers, open pipeline, and HubSpot engagement — the same live data as the Pre-Event Analysis tab on the full internal dashboard, on its own page.
Methodology ?
• Pre-Event Analysis: cross-references the uploaded AI Infra Summit 2026 attendee/speaker roster (209 companies, 280 people) against a live, uncapped pull of every ABM-tiered Salesforce Account, matched by normalized company name (punctuation and common legal suffixes stripped) — not domain, since the roster has none. "Open Opportunity" = not a Closed stage, Type not renewal-like, Close Date in the current calendar year; this is a best-effort reproduction of a typical open-pipeline filter, not a guaranteed match to any specific saved report. "Event Family" lightly groups a few known raw Campaign Names (RAISE 2025/2026, PyTorch 2025, Webinars) — a hand-maintained pattern list, not a Salesforce field. Company-name matching is inherently fuzzier than domain-based matching — treat unmatched companies as a starting point for manual review, not a definitive gap list.
• Pipeline default value is $200,000 per account with no real open Opportunity.
• Data refreshes automatically every 6 hours via the clockwork-sfdc-sync-aiinfra26 GitHub Action; re-open this page to see the latest snapshot as of load time.
• Attendee Warmth: cross-references attending accounts against a portal-wide HubSpot Marketing Qualified Account (MQA) rollup, joined by the real Salesforce Account Id. A Company is an MQA once at least one associated Contact has reached HubSpot lifecyclestage Marketing Qualified Lead or later. "Qualified Since" / "Last Activity" come from each account's single best (highest-stage) HubSpot contact — a live check of this portal found ~98% of MQL+ contacts have no tracked marketing engagement (no form conversions, source = Offline), so these dates are the closest reliable "is anyone engaging with this account" signal available, not a marketing-attribution trail.
