Three tabs: Pre-Event Analysis (attendee roster cross-referenced against ABM tiers + open pipeline, ahead of the event), Event & Post-Event (live booth-scan funnel, once Leads start flowing in from the campaign), and Account Engagement / HubSpot (portal-wide Marketing Qualified Account rollup from HubSpot contact activity, joined to Salesforce ABM tiers).
Methodology ?
• Pre-Event Analysis: cross-references the uploaded AI Infra Summit 2026 attendee/speaker roster (209 companies, 280 people) against a live, uncapped pull of every ABM-tiered Salesforce Account, matched by normalized company name (punctuation and common legal suffixes stripped) — not domain, since the roster has none. "Open Opportunity" = not a Closed stage, Type not renewal-like, Close Date in the current calendar year; this is a best-effort reproduction of a typical open-pipeline filter, not a guaranteed match to any specific saved report. "Event Family" lightly groups a few known raw Campaign Names (RAISE 2025/2026, PyTorch 2025, Webinars) — a hand-maintained pattern list, not a Salesforce field. Company-name matching is inherently fuzzier than the domain-based matching used elsewhere on this page — treat unmatched companies as a starting point for manual review, not a definitive gap list.
• Company Already Engaged (Event & Post-Event tab) = Salesforce shows an existing Opportunity (any owner/team), an existing Contact, or another open Lead tied to that company's domain, from OUTSIDE this campaign's leads — i.e., someone at that company was already talking to us before/regardless of who we met via this campaign.
• Qualified = real Salesforce Lead Status of "In Progress (MQL)" or "In Progress (SAL)" — not an estimate.
• Net New Opportunity requires Lead Source exactly "AI Infra Summit 2026 Santa Clara" AND Created Date on/after 2026-09-15; everything else domain-matched is classified "Existing" pipeline.
• Pipeline default value is $200,000 per account with no real open Opportunity.
• "GTM persona" is a heuristic classification from job-title keywords, not a Salesforce field — treat as a starting point, not ground truth.
• Data refreshes automatically every 6 hours via the clockwork-sfdc-sync-aiinfra26 GitHub Action; re-open this page to see the latest snapshot as of load time.
• Year-over-Year (2025 vs 2026): 2025 figures come from a saved Salesforce report (Contacts already converted from Leads, tied to the "Booth Scans | AI Infra Summit 2025" campaign, at In Progress (SAL) or Open Deal stage) — a post-event snapshot. 2026 figures come from the live Lead/CampaignMember sync for this year's campaign, captured pre-event. Different object types (Contact vs. Lead) at different funnel stages, captured at different points in each event's lifecycle — this is directional context, not a strict apples-to-apples comparison.
• Account Engagement (HubSpot): portal-wide, not scoped to this event. A Company becomes a Marketing Qualified Account (MQA) the moment ANY associated Contact reaches HubSpot lifecyclestage Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL) or later (Sales Accepted Lead, SQL, Opportunity, Customer) — this HubSpot portal has no native numeric lead-scoring field, so the lifecycle-stage funnel IS the scoring methodology, and this status is a ratchet (doesn't revert if a contact's stage later drops). Joined to Salesforce Accounts by domain (Company.domain vs. Account.Website), same technique used elsewhere on this page — accounts with no domain match show as "Not Matched to Salesforce". Refreshes on the same 6-hour schedule as the rest of this page (clockwork-sfdc-sync-aiinfra26 GitHub Action, sync_hubspot.py).
• Attendee Warmth (Pre-Event): cross-references attending accounts (Pre-Event Analysis tab) against the portal-wide HubSpot MQA rollup, joined by the real Salesforce Account Id (both sides are already resolved to the same Account, so no fuzzy matching here) — shows how warm attending accounts already are in HubSpot walking in, independent of any booth-scan activity at this event.
